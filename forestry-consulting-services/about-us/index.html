About Us

Integrating Technology into Land Management

Founder David Edwards is a forester with over 30 years of experience in a broad array of forestry and aviation-related enterprises. He holds a Forest Management degree from Oregon State University and earned his “boots on the ground” field forestry education in Oregon’s Coast Range.

His background includes mission-focused military and civilian aviation, leading wildfire aviation operations, integrating advanced technology into land management, and extensive experience with unmanned aerial systems (UAS).
